Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Voice & Audio Recorder (“the Extension”) is built by Fenwick Labs. We design it to keep your audio on your device.

What we collect

Recording audio is processed locally in Chrome. Your recordings are saved to your Downloads folder. We do not upload call audio for transcription or cloud storage.

Optional account

If you sign in to purchase or restore Lifetime Pro, we may store account and entitlement status through our authentication provider. That data is used only to verify your plan.

Local preferences

The Extension stores small preferences locally (theme, capture mode, monthly usage count, and cached entitlement) using Chrome storage APIs.
