FAQ

Straight answers.

Does my audio leave my computer?

No. Recordings stay on your device and download locally. Optional sign-in is only for plan verification.

How does the free limit work?

You get 10 recordings each calendar month. The count resets on the first of the next month, not from your install date.

What does Lifetime include?

Lifetime Pro removes the monthly recording limit on this browser. There is no monthly or yearly tier — only the one-time plan.

Can recording continue if I close the popup?

Yes. Recording keeps running until you tap End & Save, or until Chrome or your PC closes — then it autosaves when possible.
