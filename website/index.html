FAQ

Clear answers before you upgrade.

Does my audio leave my computer?

No. Recordings stay on your device and download locally. Google sign-in is only used to verify Lifetime Pro.

Why do I need Google for Lifetime?

Lifetime Pro is tied to your Google account so your plan can be restored on this browser. Free recording still works without signing in.

How does the free limit work?

You get 10 recordings each calendar month. The count resets on the first of the next month.

Is there a monthly or yearly plan?

No. Voice & Audio Recorder only offers Free and a one-time Lifetime Pro unlock at $20.

Can recording continue if I close the popup?

Yes. Recording keeps running until you tap End & Save, or until Chrome closes — then it autosaves when possible.
